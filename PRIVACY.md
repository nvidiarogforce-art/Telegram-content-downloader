# Privacy Policy

Effective: 24 August 2026

Telegram Video Saver does not collect, sell, transmit, or retain personal data, browsing history, Telegram messages, or downloaded videos.

The extension runs only on `https://web.telegram.org/*`. It reads rendered video elements solely to identify videos the user can already access and to perform a user-requested download. The enabled state is saved with Chrome's Storage API. A requested video's bytes are fetched, validated, and saved locally inside the Telegram tab; they are not sent to an extension server.

The extension contains no analytics, advertising SDK, remote code, telemetry, or external extension backend. Chrome and Telegram may process data under their own policies as part of their normal browser and web-service operation.

Questions or security reports can be submitted through this repository's GitHub Issues page without including private Telegram content.
